=============================================================================
OUTPUT INTERPRETATION
=============================================================================

From smoother.certify():

certified_map ∈ {-1, 0, 1}^{H×W}
  1  = Certified important (p_1 >= τ)
  0  = Certified unimportant (p_0 >= τ)
  -1 = Abstain (insufficient confidence)

p_1 ∈ [0,1]^{H×W}
  Probability the pixel is important

p_0 ∈ [0,1]^{H×W}
  Probability the pixel is unimportant

certified_radius
  R = σ · Φ^(-1)(τ)
  Robustness guarantee: perturbations ||δ||_2 < R
  preserve the certified map

pct_certified
  Percentage of pixels with cert ≠ -1

pct_abstained
  Percentage of pixels with cert = -1
